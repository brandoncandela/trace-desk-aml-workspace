# Trace Desk application surface

## Scope

The single-page Trace Desk AML Workspace at `static/index.html` and its generated `dist/index.html` build.

## Visitor mode

Operate.

## Audience and job

AML analysts collect or open a bounded case, inspect evidence, document judgment, and prepare a reviewable handoff. QC reviewers verify the submitted scope, evidence, reasoning, and disposition. Recruiters may explore the saved example as a portfolio work sample.

## Constraints

Preserve every current feature, calculation, data source, export, test, and GitHub Pages behavior. Do not imply authenticated workflow controls, persistent server storage, wallet attribution, or automated compliance decisions.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A case review should feel like a disciplined investigation file, not a promotional dashboard. The surface replaces the stacked card wall with a clear application shell, persistent case context, and focused work areas.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep ink navigation and light slate work surfaces create an institutional research desk. One teal accent marks active investigation state; amber is reserved for limitations and attention. Square-edged panels, quiet rules, tabular numerals, and compact evidence controls replace decorative gradients, glow, and pill repetition.

**STORY:** The analyst immediately sees what the product does, how to start or open a case, and the four review stages. Once evidence loads, current case scope remains visible while the analyst moves from network review to evidence, assessment, and QC.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact dark application bar sits above a light work surface. The title and short purpose lead on the left; a unified Start or open a case panel sits on the right. A horizontal workflow rail and a persistent case summary establish state before detailed investigation content.

**FORM:** Code-led enterprise investigation workspace, ranked first for product clarity. seed: none (brief-pinned direction)

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
